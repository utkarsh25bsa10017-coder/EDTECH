# Existing preparation material for Prashnottar's four exams

Last checked: 25 November 2026. Covers **BPSC TRE**, **Bihar Daroga (BPSSC SI)**, **BPSC CCE Prelims** and **UP SI**.

This is a map of what aspirants already buy and use: solved past-paper (PYQ) books, sample/practice-set books, standard study books, and online alternatives. Use it to:

- find official past papers to analyse (topic weights, difficulty, pattern);
- pick the standard books our new questions should be consistent with;
- see what competitors charge and where the gaps are.

> **Rules for the content team.** Read these to understand the pattern; never copy, translate or reword their questions or solutions into Prashnottar. Our questions are written new. Only official past papers may be reproduced, with the commission credited.
>
> Book listings come from publisher and retailer pages seen on the date above. Editions, prices and stock change often: check the edition year before relying on one.

## 2. Every subject in each exam, and where to study it

Each exam's papers and subjects are listed below with the material that covers them. The book and platform names point to the detailed tables in sections 3–6.

> The structure comes from the coaching-site syllabus summaries linked under each exam. They agree on the papers and subjects but differ on question counts, timing and negative marking. Confirm those in the latest official notification before building a mock to that pattern.

### BPSC TRE (teacher recruitment)

Syllabus summaries: [Prepp](https://prepp.in/bpsc-tre-exam/syllabus), [Adda247 (primary)](https://www.adda247.com/teaching-jobs-exam/bpsc-tre-primary-teacher-syllabus), [Oliveboard](https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/bpsc-tre-4-0-syllabus-and-exam-pattern/).

The paper has three parts: **I. Language**, **II. General Studies**, and **III. the subject paper** for the post applied for. Classes 1–5 have no separate subject paper.

| Part | Subject | Study material | Practice and PYQs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. Language (qualifying) | Hindi | Lucent's Samanya Hindi; Vasudev Nandan Prasad's grammar | YCT TRE GS & Language Solved Papers; Testbook BPSC General Hindi PYQs |
| I. Language (qualifying) | English | Any standard English grammar and comprehension book | YCT TRE GS & Language Solved Papers |
| I. Language (qualifying) | Urdu, Bengali and other languages offered | Confirm the list in the notification; Bihar SCERT language textbooks | Few dedicated books; gap |
| II. General Studies | History, national movement, geography, polity, economy | NCERT class 6–12; Lucent's GK | YCT and Adda247 TRE GS solved papers |
| II. General Studies | General science | NCERT class 6–10 science; Lucent's GK | YCT and Adda247 TRE GS solved papers |
| II. General Studies | Bihar GK | Arihant Bihar Samanya Gyan; Know Your State Bihar | KGS and Adda247 Bihar material |
| II. General Studies | Current affairs (national, international, Bihar) | Monthly current affairs magazines; Bihar budget and Economic Survey | Adda247 and Testbook current affairs quizzes |
| II. General Studies | Mental ability and maths | R.S. Aggarwal's Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning | Adda247 TRE mocks |
| II. General Studies | Child development and pedagogy (where the level includes it) | CTET pedagogy guides; NCF 2005; RTE Act 2009 | TRE solved papers; CTET PYQ books |
| III. Classes 1–5 | Maths & science, social science, Hindi, English, Urdu, Sanskrit and the other options listed | NCERT and Bihar SCERT class 1–8 books | Adda247 PRT mocks; PW PRT batch |
| III. Classes 6–8 | Maths & science, social science, Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Urdu and the other options listed | NCERT and Bihar SCERT class 6–10 books | Adda247 class 6–8 mocks |
| III. Classes 9–10 | Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Urdu, mathematics, science, social science, and the other subjects in the notification | NCERT and Bihar SCERT class 9–12 books | YCT subject solved papers (e.g. Sanskrit 9–10); Adda247 subject mocks |
| III. Classes 11–12 | Physics, chemistry, mathematics, botany, zoology, history, geography, economics, political science, sociology, psychology, philosophy, Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Urdu, accountancy, business studies, computer science, home science, music, fine arts, and the other subjects in the notification | NCERT class 11–12 plus a standard graduation-level text for the subject | Adda247 PGT mocks per subject (e.g. history PGT book, maths, computer science, philosophy series) |

The exact subject list for classes 6–12 changes with each notification's vacancies. Take it from the subject-wise vacancy table in the current advertisement on [bpsc.bihar.gov.in](https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in).

Syllabus summaries: [Adda247](https://www.adda247.com/exams/bihar/bihar-police-si-syllabus), [Testbook](https://testbook.com/bihar-police-si/syllabus-exam-pattern), [Khan Global Studies](https://khanglobalstudies.com/blog/bihar-police-si-syllabus-and-exam-pattern-2024).

Don't confuse this exam with the separate **Prohibition SI** exam, which has its own pattern.

### UP SI (UP Police Sub-Inspector)

Syllabus summaries: [Adda247](https://adda247.com/exams/up/up-si-syllabus), [Careers360](https://competition.careers360.com/articles/up-police-si-syllabus), [PW Store](https://store.pw.live/blogs/uppsc-exams/up-police-si-syllabus-and-exam-pattern), [Free Press Journal (2026)](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/up-si-syllabus-2026-check-subject-wise-syllabus-breakdown-marking-system-exam-pattern-and-selection-process).

The written exam has four sections of 100 marks each (400 in total).

| Section | Subject | Study material | Practice and PYQs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. General Hindi | Grammar, vocabulary, idioms, tatsam-tadbhav, Hindi literature and authors | Lucent's Samanya Hindi; Yukti UP Police SI Samanya Hindi; Vasudev Nandan Prasad's grammar | YCT UP SI & ASI Solved Papers; Arihant 45 Solved Papers |
| 2. Basic Law | Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA); laws protecting women, children and Scheduled Castes/Tribes; traffic rules; environment and wildlife protection; human rights; RTI Act | Bare acts on India Code; a BNS/BNSS guide written for police exams | YCT and Arihant solved papers (older papers ask IPC/CrPC, which the new codes replaced in 2024) |
| 2. Constitution | Fundamental rights and duties, DPSP, parliament, judiciary, important articles | Laxmikanth's Indian Polity | Same solved papers |
| 2. General Knowledge | Indian history, geography, economy, science, current affairs | NCERT; Lucent's GK; current affairs magazines | Same solved papers |
| 2. UP GK | UP history, geography, culture, economy, schemes | Shiv Kumar Ojha's Uttar Pradesh GK (Pariksha Vani) | Same solved papers |
| 3. Numerical & Mental Ability | Arithmetic: percentage, ratio, average, interest, time-speed-distance, time and work, profit and loss, number system | R.S. Aggarwal's Quantitative Aptitude; Quickest Mathematics | YCT and Arihant solved papers |
| 4. Mental Aptitude | Police-related aptitude: law and order, communal harmony, crime control, rule of law, public interest, police system | Situational-judgement sections of UP SI solved papers | Arihant UP SI Mental Ability & Reasoning Solved Papers |
| 4. Intelligence & Reasoning | Series, analogy, coding, directions, blood relations, logical diagrams, symbolic relations, perception | R.S. Aggarwal's Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning | Arihant UP SI Mental Ability & Reasoning Solved Papers |

The law section is the biggest risk for old material: anything printed before July 2024 teaches IPC, CrPC and the Evidence Act, which BNS, BNSS and BSA have replaced.

## 7. What this means for Prashnottar

- **Books are cheap and everywhere.** A solved-paper book costs ₹250–550. Our ₹1,000 Pass must win on what a book can't do: timed computer-based mocks, instant explanations, weak-topic reports, and covering all four exams.
- **Big apps sell breadth, not depth.** Adda247 and Testbook cover every exam, but their Bihar- and UP-specific material is thin and mostly bilingual by translation. A Hindi-first, exam-specific product is the gap.
